## Logging, round by round

Every round is its own entry: one tap per set, mid-workout. Lifts, runs and stations land in the same log, so a heavy squat day and Thursday's intervals feed one load model, and every set counts on the muscles that did it.

## Health data from the watch

A Garmin connection pulls daily HRV, resting heart rate, steps, sleep score and sleep duration. Each shows its trend, with a 90-day chart behind it. Heart-rate zones come from the watch's max and threshold heart rate.

The watch adds to the log; it doesn't replace it. You log the workout you did. Afterwards the run or ride attaches to the block it belongs to: distance, pace, heart rate, zones and laps. What you entered stays yours; the watch fills in what it measured.

## 100k+ cycles, solved ahead

A training cycle is planned for the days you can train, the minutes you have and your goal, for Hyrox, hybrid, CrossFit or bodybuilding. Each plan is a constraint solve (CP-SAT). Instead of solving while you wait, the solve runs offline for every rounded combination of inputs, and more than 100,000 cycles are stored. Picking one reads the stored plan; your equipment, injuries and loads are then fitted to it.

## A grammar for workouts

The workout generator does not pick from a list. It counts the patterns in real workouts (formats, movements, how rounds and caps are built) and writes new ones from them, for running, Hyrox, hybrid and CrossFit. Every workout passes checks before it is shown: doable, the right equipment, within its time cap. Each carries its scores. The generator fills planned sessions, offers swaps in the app, and answers your AI assistant through MCP.

## Ask your AI assistant for a workout

Goblin Gains is an MCP server. Ask Claude, ChatGPT or another assistant for a workout and it asks the generator: the workouts come from the library and its coaching rules, not from the AI. Each comes with a link to save it in the app and log it. It is free and needs no account.

```text
https://api.goblingains.com/mcp
```

- **Claude (claude.ai and the apps):** Settings, then Connectors, then Add custom connector. Name it goblingains, paste the address, leave sign-in off.
- **Claude Code:**

  ```sh
  claude mcp add --transport http \
    goblingains https://api.goblingains.com/mcp
  ```

- **ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code, Gemini CLI:** the steps for each are on <a href="https://goblingains.com/ai">goblingains.com/ai</a>.

Without a key, everyone on the same assistant shares an hourly limit. With an account, make a key in the app under Profile, then AI assistants, and send it as `Authorization: Bearer gg_mcp_…`. Then ask, for example: "A 20-minute workout with a rower and one kettlebell" or "Fran, but as an EMOM".
