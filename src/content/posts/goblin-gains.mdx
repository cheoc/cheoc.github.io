## Every set lands on the muscles that did it

Squats, lunges and the wall balls inside a WOD all count toward your quads. The map shows what training has loaded and what is fresh, muscle by muscle, front and back, so the next session is chosen on evidence instead of feel.

## The watch adds to the log; it doesn't replace it

You log the workout you did. Afterwards the run or ride from a Garmin attaches to the block it belongs to: distance, pace, heart rate and zones. What you entered stays yours; the watch fills in what it measured.
